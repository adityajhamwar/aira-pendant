# AIRA – Hardware Bill of Materials (Prototype 1)

## Main Controller Board
**Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense** – ~€15.39  
Thumb-sized brain with dual-core 240MHz processor, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, 8MB PSRAM, 8MB FLASH, built-in mic, and battery charging circuit.

## External Audio Input
**INMP441 MEMS I²S Microphone Module** – ~€5.63  
High-performance digital MEMS microphone with 24-bit I²S output for clear voice capture. Buy a 5-pack for spares.

## Visual Status Indicator
**WS2812B Addressable RGB LED (NeoPixel-compatible)** – ~€0.50  
Single addressable RGB LED. Glows blue (listening), green (recording), red (low battery). 5×5mm SMD package.

## Power Supply
**3.7V 500mAh Li-Po Battery (JST-PH 2.0)** – ~€4.75  
Slim rechargeable battery with built-in protection circuit. 4–5 hours active streaming.

**TP4056 USB-C Charging Module (with DW01 Protection)** – ~€2.39  
Single-cell Li-Ion charger with USB-C input. Full charge in ~40 minutes.

## Physical Control
**6×6mm Tactile Push Button** – ~€0.10  
Push-to-talk trigger connected directly to GPIO.

## Wiring & Connections
- **28 AWG Silicone Wire (Red & Black)** – ~€5.22  
- **Heat Shrink Tubing Kit (150 pcs)** – ~€4.88  
- **M3 Nylon Screw & Standoff Kit (260 pcs)** – ~€6.58

## Enclosure Material
**Translucent TPU Filament (1.75mm, 750g)** – ~€28.79  
Flexible, durable filament for 3D printing the pendant shell.

## Prototyping Tools
- **Solderless Breadboard Kit with Jumper Wires** – ~€12.15  
- **60W Soldering Iron Kit with Multimeter (25 pcs)** – ~€23.14

**Total Prototype Cost: ~€117.28**
